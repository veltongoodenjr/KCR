Kingston City Run 2027

Save the Date: Sunday, March 14th 2027!

Early Bird Registration Opening Soon!

Kingston City Run celebrates its 10th Anniversary! Come Run, walk, or wheel with us in Kingston the heartbeat of Jamaica! Support our charities and run for a cause! The Kingston City Run, brings together international and local runners, communities and corporations for a once in a lifetime experience celebrating the mystic, cultural and musical vibrations of this magnificent city! Our scenic route takes you through and by some of the cities top attractions and places of interest including exclusive venus that only Kingston City Run features. We invite ALL runners who started with us in 2013 until now to come, reunite, celebrate and make new memories with us and other first time runners.

Your participation in also key in raising funds for Kingston's most vulnerable communities.

Why Join the Adventure?

Multiple Race Events

Take on the 10K run or join the accessible 5K Walk/Run/Wheel—open to all ages and abilities.

Kingston Pirate’s Week

Immerse yourself in the excitement of Kingston Pirate’s Week with parades, music, costumes, and city-wide events.

Help Raise for Charity

Proceeds support Kingston’s homeless charities and families rebuilding after Hurricane Melissa.

Team & Family Fun

Corporate challenges, family teams, and community groups are all welcome. Celebrate teamwork and community spirit.

Rep Your School Challenge

High school and tertiary students get a J$1,500 5K entry. Enter as a school team, compete for cash prizes, bragging rights, and pirate vibes.
